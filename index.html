Kyntra Bio, Inc. · Nasdaq: KYNB · formerly FibroGen, Inc.

Novel therapies in oncology and rare disease.

Kyntra Bio is FibroGen under a new name. After selling its China business to AstraZeneca in 2025, it says it is focused on two programs: FG-3246, an antibody-drug conjugate in a Phase 2 trial for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, and roxadustat for anemia in lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes, where it plans a Phase 3 trial. Neither is approved for those uses.

Listing status, filed October 6, 2026

Nasdaq staff determined to delist Kyntra Bio’s common stock from the Nasdaq Global Select Market after the company had not shown compliance with Listing Rule 5450(b) by September 29. The company has requested a hearing. The stock stays listed and trading while a Nasdaq Hearings Panel decides. Read the details 8-K, Oct 6

The company

FibroGen became Kyntra Bio between February 2025 and October 2026

A sale, a reverse split, a new name, a bankruptcy abroad, a renegotiated financing and a listing notice, each from a dated filing. The company had 34 employees in the U.S. at December 31, 2025 and is based in San Francisco.

  5. Jan 7, 2026

    Nasdaq told the company it no longer met the $50 million total assets and total revenue test of Listing Rule 5450(b)(3)(A), because the China revenue sits in discontinued operations. It also did not meet the equity or market-value alternatives.

    The subsidiary voluntarily filed for bankruptcy in Finland, and a Helsinki court declared it bankrupt on April 16, so Kyntra Bio deconsolidated it. Anemia

Roxadustat: from kidney disease to MDS

Roxadustat is an oral HIF-PH inhibitor approved in Europe, Japan, China and other countries for anemia in chronic kidney disease. Kyntra Bio plans a Phase 3 trial in anemia of lower-risk MDS, a use for which it is investigational. It holds the rights in the U.S., Canada, Mexico and markets not held by AstraZeneca or licensed to Astellas, which sells it as EVRENZO in Europe and Japan. The 10-K says roxadustat is not approved for commercialization in any indication in the U.S., Canada or Mexico, the territories Kyntra Bio retains; China was sold to AstraZeneca in 2025. 10-Q, MD&A 10-K, Item 1

Legal and indemnification

An SEC proceeding against a former officer

There is one SEC complaint against the company’s former Chief Medical Officer in U.S. District Court. The company is not a party, but under its indemnification agreement it must advance certain legal expenses and may have to indemnify certain costs and judgments. Some judgments, settlements or fines sought by the SEC would not be covered. Accrued liabilities for these obligations were about $7.0 million at June 30, 2026, up from not material at December 31, 2025. The 10-Q says the costs of this proceeding are now material and that the company’s insurance has exclusions and caps; the company says it cannot reasonably estimate costs beyond the accrual.

Separately, non-cancelable purchase obligations were $5.7 million for roxadustat manufacture and $1.0 million for general purchases. 10-Q, note 10
